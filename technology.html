The Technology

Fermented oat actives, engineered for the skin, not just the surface.

Disposable gloves protect against chemical, biological, and physical hazards, but the washing, sanitizing, and prolonged occlusion that come with wearing them take a real toll on skin. Avenall™ was built to close that gap.

A novel, patent-pending extract

OEI Glove Coating Actives is a novel fermented extract derived from Avena sativa. Fermentation unlocks bioactive compounds (naturally occurring vitamins, minerals, antioxidants, and oat pre- and post-biotics) that are formulated directly into the glove coating, ready to work the moment the glove goes on.

It's the first meaningful advance in glove coating science in over two decades, going further than powders and basic moisturizers ever could.
