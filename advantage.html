The Avenall Advantage™

A holistic approach: from clock in to clock out.

Glove coating actives are only part of the story. The Avenall Advantage™ pairs the coating technology with a complementary skincare regimen, so skin is looked after before, during, and after every shift.

Before Your Shift

Prep & Protect

Avenall™ barrier sanitizers and lotions prime skin before gloves go on, so hands start the shift feeling balanced and hydrated instead of already dry and tight.

During Your Shift

Continuous Care

Every glove is coated with OEI Glove Coating Actives, working with the skin for as long as the gloves are worn, not just at the moment of application.

After Your Shift

Replenish & Renew

Nourishing Avenall™ lotion replenishes moisture, leaving skin feeling softer and more comfortable before the next shift begins.

Why It Matters

One coating can't do it alone.

Avenall™ is built around nitrile gloves designed for the medical and healthcare space, worn alongside a hand sanitizer and a lotion as a complete three-part system. Wearing disposable gloves is only one part of a much longer routine; hands are washed and sanitized before donning, worn under occlusion for hours, then stripped bare again at the end of a shift. Addressing any single moment in isolation leaves gaps.

Each piece of the system is formulated for a specific role in that routine, and the three are designed to work best together.

Before · Sanitizer

Supports Skin Integrity

Alcohol-based sanitizing is necessary, but it can leave skin feeling dry and stripped. Avenall™ Barrier Sanitizer, formulated with oat-derived ingredients, cleanses while helping skin stay resilient against that drying effect.

During · Glove

Supports a Balanced Complexion

Worn for the length of a shift, the Avenall™ glove coating is designed to help maintain the skin's natural balance, for a smoother, healthier-looking complexion throughout the day.

After · Lotion

Replenishes Moisture & Comfort

At the end of a shift, nourishing Avenall™ lotion replenishes moisture and helps skin feel calm, soft, and comfortable again.

Used on its own, each product does its job well. Used together, they're designed to support skin through the entire arc of a shift, not just one moment in it.
