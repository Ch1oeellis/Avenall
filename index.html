The Problem

Healthcare workers' hands frequently end up dry, rough, and uncomfortable during and after shifts.

Constant hand washing strips away the skin's natural oils, and sanitizing dries hands out even further. On top of that, gloves create a warm, damp, occlusive environment that leaves skin feeling tight and uncomfortable by the end of a shift.

Avenall™ understands these challenges and developed a complete system to keep hands looking and feeling soft, smooth, and comfortable using our botanical formulations.

About Avenall™

Our Story and Mission

Avenall™ begin with our Grandfather, Joe Neuser who was an inventor, real-estate developer, teacher, and leader at heart. This company was his retirement brain-child, though we joke he was never really retired and always had a project. Throughout nearly a decade of developing various patented formulas with his business partners, he was working towards the next generation of oat derived skincare. His abrupt passing left our family responsible for carrying on his vision and honoring his legacy. Now run by his grandchildren, Jackson, Payton, Ryan, and Chloe, we are destined to continue his work and build Avenall.

Our name comes from Avena sativa, the common oat, and our work starts in the same place: taking a botanical that has been part of skincare for centuries and making it work harder for the people who wear gloves all day.

Our patent-pending process produces advanced oat extracts with actives roughly 2,000 times smaller than colloidal oatmeal. We build them into disposable glove coatings and a complementary line of soap, sanitizer, and lotion, and we partner with glove manufacturers and healthcare facilities to bring them to hardworking hands.
