The Problem

Gloves protect the job. They don't protect your skin.

Repeated hand washing and sanitizing strips natural oils from the skin, and once gloves go on, moisture builds up underneath with nowhere to go. The result: skin that can look and feel dry, tight, and uncomfortable by the end of every shift.
